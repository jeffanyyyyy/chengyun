01 — About Us

Cheng — clarity, made transparent.
Yun — the ground worked, step by step.

In this industry, asymmetry of information is the most expensive cost of all. Companies spend heavily and still get a rough result, because the work has been subcontracted down through layer after layer; meanwhile the performers on stage and the technicians on the front line meet squeezed rates and deductions nobody explains. Chengyun was founded to change that.

We run on the discipline of corporate management, with a modular, transparent pricing system that breaks event costs and service specifications down scientifically — giving corporate clients a clear, standardised process with real control over budget. Our work spans large corporate annual events and brand launches, high-end visual and physical craft design, integration of local artisans and art IP, and low-carbon event delivery.

We work founder-led, backed by a network of senior specialists. Precise project control and a deep visual standard carry a project from planning through specification to delivery on site — one team, end to end.

However lavish an event looks, without sound trust and dignity in labour it is a tower built on sand. Chengyun stands on integrity and transparency, and on execution done slowly and carefully, so that every brand appearance creates both the highest return and lasting value.

Core Values
Transparency & Integrity
No black boxes. Modular, transparent pricing, against an industry habit of asymmetry and vague quotes.
Craftsmanship & Precision
Management SOP as the skeleton; visual aesthetics, materials and on-site detail held to a strict standard. Each event is worked like a made object.
Sustainability & Shared Value
ESG thinking applied to events, turning commercial activity into an ecosystem that supports local artisans and the places they work in.
Humanity & Respect
A stage for creators, and fair pay, safety and dignity for the people working the front line — a commercial path with warmth in it.

05 — Q&A

What stallholders ask most.

Is talking really not allowed?

Silent interaction comes first: please use the hand-held signs, cards and gestures we provide. Loudspeakers, microphones and sales patter are strictly prohibited. When a crowd arrives, serving the customer takes priority and quiet conversation is fine.

Do we bring our own tent, table and chairs?

No. We provide the tent (3 x 1.5 m, shared by two stalls), one table (approx. 120 x 60 cm), one chair, a custom brand sign and ambient string lights.

How much power does each stall get?

Basic lighting and device power are free, capped at 200 W per stall. Undeclared high-draw heating, cooking or high-output lighting equipment is not permitted.

What time is load-in and load-out?

Check-in 14:00–15:00, with set-up finished by 14:50. The market runs 15:00–20:30. Load-out is 20:30–21:30, with the site cleared and restored by 21:30.

How does the early-bird discount work?

Apply and pay in full before 10 September 2026 for 5% off the total. Brands joining three or more sessions also receive a custom brand puzzle, redeemable against future stall fees.

Can we get a refund if we can't make it?

Cancel 14 days or more before the date: full refund less NT$200 in handling and transfer fees. 7–13 days before: 50% refunded. Within 6 days, including the day itself: no refund or transfer.

For anything else, message us on IG / FB / LINE.
